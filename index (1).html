01 · About

About

I'm Fribereece, a freelance data systems and analytics practitioner. Most of my clients arrive with the same problem: important data lives in one overloaded sheet, and every report means hours of copying, fixing, and double-checking.

I restructure that data into a clean master source, then build the order sheets, quotes, dashboards, and reports that run from it automatically. My background is in healthcare operations, so I'm comfortable with sensitive data, strict accuracy, and documentation people can follow.

Before freelancing, I spent over 10 years in healthcare laboratory operations, from patient intake to result reporting, working with EHR data, quality audits, and HIPAA-compliant workflows. That is where I learned to care about accuracy and clear handoffs.
